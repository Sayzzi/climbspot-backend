# One Ascent per side, in one direction

An uphill path is modelled as an **Ascent**: it always goes up, from its Start to its Top, and each side of a mountain is a separate Ascent (Mont Ventoux from Bédoin, Malaucène and Sault are three Ascents). Metrics such as Gradient only make sense in one direction, and runners and cyclists choose a side, not a mountain. Grouping the sides of one high point is left to a future **Summit** concept.

We deliberately avoid the word "climb" in the domain, even though the product is named ClimbSpot and cyclists say "climb": climbing sites (crags, gyms, routes, boulders) are planned, and "climb" would become ambiguous. "Hill" was rejected because it names the landform rather than a way up it. User-facing copy may still say "climb" or "hill" through localisation keys.
